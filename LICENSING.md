# Licensing

x1agent is source-available under the Business Source License 1.1 (BSL 1.1). The full legal text lives in [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE). This page is the plain-English summary.

## What you can do today

You can read the source, modify it, fork it, run it on your own infrastructure, and use it inside your company. You can run it for paying customers as part of a larger product or service, as long as you are not offering x1agent itself as the product.

You do not need to ask permission. You do not need a commercial agreement. The license grants these rights up front.

## What you cannot do today

You cannot offer x1agent as a hosted or managed service that competes with the commercial offering from DEV360 LLC. "Offering x1agent as a service" means letting third parties use x1agent's functionality through an interface you operate, without them installing it themselves.

If your use case looks like that and you want to do it anyway, contact licensing@x1agent.com for a commercial license.

## What changes on the change date

The change date is **2029-05-05** (three years after this license was applied).

On that date, the version of x1agent covered by this license automatically converts to **Apache License 2.0**. From then on, anyone can do anything Apache 2.0 permits, including offering competing hosted services.

Each release carries its own change date. A release made today converts on 2029-05-05. A release made next year converts in 2030. The license rolls forward with new releases; old releases keep their original change date.

## Why BSL

We picked BSL 1.1 because it solves one specific problem: a large cloud vendor taking the project, hosting it as a managed service, and capturing the commercial value without contributing back. BSL prevents that for three years and then opens fully.

For everyone else — operators self-hosting, contributors, companies embedding x1agent inside their own products — BSL behaves like an open license from day one.

This is the same model used by Sentry, MariaDB, CockroachDB, and others.

## Contributing

Contributions to x1agent are accepted under the same license as the project itself. By submitting a pull request you agree your contribution can be relicensed under Apache 2.0 on the change date along with the rest of the project.
